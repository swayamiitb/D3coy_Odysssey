The honest test: we took the 110 flaws CISA confirmed were attacked between August and October 2026 and asked what each triage rule would have flagged. EPSS of 0.1 or more would have missed 49 percent, even using today’s scores, which already include hindsight. CVSS 9 or higher would have missed 53 percent, because severity is not exploitation. Being on CISA’s list is one of our own rules, so our 110 out of 110 is by design, and we say so. The meaningful number is how many were also caught by a second channel: CISA’s SSVC data or the vendor’s own “exploited in the wild” wording. One number is never enough, so we combine the evidence.
