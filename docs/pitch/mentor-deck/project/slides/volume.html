Three numbers, one story. 16,730 new flaws in a single month is far more than any team can read. NVD now labels only about one in five with the product and version, so most arrive half-labelled. And 29% of exploited flaws were attacked on or before announcement day, so waiting for the label means waiting too long. The fix is not more data. It is four quick answers.
