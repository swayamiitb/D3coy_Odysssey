One unpatched server took 300 banks offline.

In July 2024, ransomware got in through an unpatched Jenkins server (a common software-building tool) at Brontoo Technology Solutions, a partner of C-Edge Technologies. About 300 small Indian banks temporarily lost UPI and ATM services.

Jan 2024

Jenkins flaw CVE-2024-23897 disclosed. Public attack code follows within days.

31 Jul 2024

Ransomware hits about 300 banks through Brontoo.

19 Aug 2024

CISA adds the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) list.

The warning signs were public for six months. CISA listed the flaw 19 days after the banks went down.

Sources: Jenkins security advisory, 24 Jan 2024 · NPCI statement, 31 Jul 2024 · CloudSEK analysis · CISA KEV

3

This is why India needs its own early warning. The Jenkins flaw had public attack code in January 2024. In July, ransomware used it at Brontoo, a partner of C-Edge, and about 300 cooperative and regional banks lost UPI and ATM services for a while. CISA only added it to its exploited list on 19 August, after the damage. A system watching the evidence, not waiting for a list, would have raised it months earlier. India’s rules already demand speed: CERT-In wants incidents reported within 6 hours.
