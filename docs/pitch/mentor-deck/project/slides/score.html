A score you can check by hand.

Risk = 100 × Relevance × Importance × (55% Likelihood + 30% Severity + 15% Evidence)

The AI never sets the score.

Fixed rules do, and every score shows its working, like a fare breakdown.

Priority (Act, Attend, Track) follows CISA’s SSVC decision method.

Confidence is scored separately: each source gets an A–F reliability grade (the Admiralty scale), and agreement between independent sources raises it.

Read it like a fare breakdown. Likelihood, severity and evidence are weighted 55, 30 and 15. Relevance asks whether the flaw applies to this exact system, and importance asks how critical that system is: a power-grid controller matters more than a test server. Both multiply, so an irrelevant flaw scores zero however scary it sounds. The AI never decides the number. Fixed rules do, and every score shows how it was calculated. Priority comes from CISA’s SSVC method.
