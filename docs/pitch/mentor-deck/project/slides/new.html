Four things set us apart. First, the decoy grid: in our simulation a sudden jump in scanning for one product raised an alarm 12 days before the flaw was announced. We tuned it to GreyNoise’s published research, which found attack surges come a median 11 days before disclosure. Second, the AI is fact-checked: a summary with one wrong number, version, deadline or authority is rejected. Third, every decision goes into a hash-chained log, so tampering is detected. Fourth, a small Bayesian network estimates whether a system is already breached, from exposure, exploitation, reachability and telemetry, and says hunt or report and evict. Fifth, it is built for India: a CERT-In 6-hour report draft, RBI and DPDP reporting clocks, routing to sector CSIRTs and NCIIPC, and briefs in all 22 scheduled Indian languages.
