Four things set us apart. First, the decoy grid: in our simulation a sudden jump in scanning for one product raised an alarm 12 days before the flaw was announced. We tuned it to GreyNoise’s published research, which found attack surges come a median 11 days before disclosure. Second, the AI is fact-checked: a summary that invents a number or a hostname is rejected. Third, every decision goes into a hash-chained log, so tampering is detected. Fourth, it is built for India: a CERT-In report draft with the 6-hour countdown, routing to sector CSIRTs and NCIIPC, and STIX export.
