05 · The use case

One advisory, start to finish

Siemens WinCC · CISA ICS advisory · real

01

A real advisory arrives

CISA’s ICS advisory for Siemens WinCC, read by the security LLM. It works even when the advisory carries no CVE id.

02

Versions parsed

Machine-readable ranges lifted from the text: vers:intdot/<8.0.0.11 and vers:all/*.

03

Exposure found

13 WinCC assets at 7 operators, checked against the inventory version by version. Patched hosts are marked safe.

04

Risk assigned

A score with its working, an SSVC tier, a CERT-In deadline. Patch where a fix exists, mitigation where none does.

05

People decide

A notice proposed to each operator in its own language. An analyst approves. Every step lands in the audit trail.

This is the walk-through in the demo. A real CISA advisory for Siemens WinCC comes in. The model reads it, the version ranges are parsed, thirteen assets at seven operators are found exposed, each gets a risk with its working and a deadline, and the notices are proposed in Marathi, Gujarati and Hindi for a person to approve. The black station is the score; the white one is the person. Then: the same path for every input.
