The problem is not a shortage of intelligence. It is volume, missing labels, and attackers who move on day zero. Each dot plate is a hundred CVEs: the lit ones are the ones that say what they break, and that share has fallen from 72 to 21 in a year; the red ones were attacked by the day they were announced. The C-Edge case is the Indian example: six months of public warning, then 300 banks offline, then the national list catching up 19 days later.
